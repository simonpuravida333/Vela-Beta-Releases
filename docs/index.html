What is Vela?

  • Vela invites you to stay in a natural environment
  • ...with infinite sky
  • ...where you can create the constellations of your mind.

Who is it for?

  • Anyone writing prose.
  • Any kind of ideation, development, composition … of words.

How it works?

  • Place stars into the sky.
  • Attach text areas to stars and start your writing journey.
